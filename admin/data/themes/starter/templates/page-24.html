Accessibility

Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to find and understand Thai company information, regardless of ability.

Last updated: June 2026

Our commitment

BizThai is committed to making its platform accessible to as many people as possible. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA and continually improve the experience for all users.

What we do

  • Use semantic HTML and ARIA labels so the site works with screen readers.
  • Support full keyboard navigation, with visible focus indicators.
  • Maintain readable colour contrast and scalable text.
  • Offer the platform in English, Thai and Chinese.

Known limitations

Some data-dense tables and interactive charts are still being improved for assistive technologies. We are actively working to close these gaps.

Give us feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, please tell us so we can fix it. Describe the page and the problem and we will respond as quickly as we can.
