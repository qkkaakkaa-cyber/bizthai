Privacy Policy

How BizThai collects, uses, and protects your information

Effective Date: June 2026

BizThai ("BizThai", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose and protect your information when you visit or use the BizThai website, services and related products.

By using our Services, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use BizThai, we may collect:

Personal Information

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Telephone Number
  • Company Name
  • Business Registration Information
  • Job Title

Technical Information

  • IP Address
  • Browser Type
  • Device Information
  • Operating System
  • Referring Website
  • Time Zone
  • Language Preference

Usage Information

  • Pages Visited
  • Search History
  • Click Activity
  • Session Duration
  • Device Identifiers

Business Information

BizThai primarily provides access to publicly available business information.

This includes information about company directors, shareholders and other officers, sourced from lawful public business registries.

Such information may constitute personal information under applicable law.

BizThai processes this information only for lawful purposes consistent with applicable laws, and applies data minimization measures, such as not making national ID or passport numbers searchable and restricting public access to director and shareholder contact details.

Individuals who are not BizThai users, but whose information appears within business records we display, may exercise the rights described under "User Rights" below, including through the Data Correction Policy referenced under "Public Business Records".

We may use information to:

  • Process correction requests
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Improve website performance
  • Respond to legal obligations
  • Protect platform security
  • Communicate with users

Cookies

BizThai uses cookies and similar technologies to:
