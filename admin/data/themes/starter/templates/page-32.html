Editorial & Data Integrity Policy

How BizThai safeguards the accuracy, independence and transparency of business information.

Effective Date: June 2026

Purpose

BizThai is committed to maintaining the accuracy, integrity, independence and transparency of the business information published on the platform.

This Editorial & Data Integrity Policy explains the principles governing the collection, review, publication and correction of business information.

Editorial Independence

Editorial decisions and data publication are made independently.

Commercial relationships, advertising, sponsorships, subscriptions or business partnerships do not influence the publication, correction or removal of business information.

Accuracy

BizThai makes reasonable efforts to ensure that published information is accurate and based on lawful sources.

Where errors are identified, BizThai will review and correct information in accordance with the Data Correction Policy.

No Paid Removal

BizThai does not accept payment in exchange for removing, hiding or suppressing lawfully published public business information.

Requests for removal are evaluated solely on the basis of applicable law, court orders, official government action or verified factual errors.

  • Required by applicable law;
  • A court order;
  • Official government action;
  • Verified factual errors.

Business Verification

Participation in the Business Verification Program does not:

  • Improve search ranking;
  • Remove public records;
  • Remove risk information;
  • Influence editorial decisions;
  • Guarantee favorable presentation.

Verification only confirms the identity or authority of the applicant.

Sponsored Content

Sponsored content or advertising will be identified where required.

Sponsored content does not represent the opinions or editorial position of BizThai.

Data Integrity

BizThai does not intentionally manipulate business information to favor or disadvantage any company.

Public records are displayed based on objective information available from lawful sources.

Corrections

Verified factual errors will be corrected after review.

BizThai may request supporting documents before making any changes.

Transparency

BizThai may publish statistics regarding:

  • Data correction requests;
  • Business verification requests;
  • Copyright complaints;
  • Legal requests.

Publication of such statistics does not disclose confidential information.
