About Our Data

Building Trust Through Transparent Business Information

Last updated: June 2026

At BizThai, we believe that transparent business information helps create a healthier business environment.

Our mission is to organize publicly available business information, improve accessibility, and help individuals and organizations make informed decisions.

Where Does Our Data Come From?

Business information displayed on BizThai may come from:

  • Public government records
  • Official business registries
  • Government Open Data
  • Official publications
  • Court publications
  • Regulatory authorities
  • Information submitted by verified companies
  • Other lawful public sources

We continuously review and update information whenever reasonably possible.

Why Is My Company Listed?

Many businesses appear on BizThai because their information has been lawfully published by government authorities or other public sources.

Our goal is to improve transparency rather than create new information.

Can I Remove My Company?

Generally, no. BizThai does not remove business information that originates from lawful public records simply because a company prefers not to appear on the platform.

However, if information is inaccurate, outdated or published in error, you may submit a correction request.

Can I Correct My Information?

Yes. Authorized company representatives may request updates or corrections by providing supporting documentation.

Our team reviews every request individually.

Do You Sell My Company's Data?

No. BizThai does not sell confidential company information.

Where commercial products or services are offered, they are provided under separate licensing terms.

Is BizThai a Government Website?

No. BizThai is an independent business information platform.

We are not affiliated with or operated by any government authority unless expressly stated.

Do Companies Pay to Remove Information?

No. BizThai does not accept payment to remove or hide lawfully published public business information.

Business partnerships and paid services never influence editorial or data integrity decisions.

Our Commitment

BizThai is committed to:

  • Transparency
  • Accuracy
  • Independence
  • Fairness
  • Responsible data practices

If you believe information is incorrect, we encourage you to contact us.
