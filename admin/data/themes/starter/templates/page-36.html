Business Verification Policy

How authorized representatives verify ownership of a company profile on BizThai

Effective Date: June 2026

BizThai provides a Business Verification Program to allow authorized representatives of a company to verify ownership of their company profile and manage certain business information available on the BizThai platform.

Purpose

Verification improves data accuracy and helps users identify official company representatives.

Eligibility

Business Verification may be requested by:

  • Company Director
  • Authorized Signatory
  • Company Owner
  • Company Secretary
  • Licensed Lawyer
  • Authorized Employee
  • Government-appointed Representative

BizThai reserves the right to request proof of authority.

Required Documents

Applicants may be required to submit:

  • Business Registration Certificate
  • Government-issued Identification
  • Passport
  • Letter of Authorization
  • Company Seal (if applicable)
  • Corporate Email Address
  • Other supporting documents

Additional verification may be required.

Verification Process

Applicants should submit a request through:

Email
support@bizthai.com

BizThai will:

  • Review submitted documents
  • Verify applicant identity
  • Confirm company authorization
  • Approve or reject the request
  • Notify the applicant by email

Verification Badge

Approved companies may receive a "Verified Business" badge displayed on their company profile.

Verification indicates only that BizThai has verified the identity or authority of the applicant.

Verification does not represent:

  • Government approval
  • Financial strength
  • Business quality
  • Legal compliance
  • Creditworthiness

Information That May Be Updated

Verified companies may request updates to:

  • Company Logo
  • Company Website
  • Email Address
  • Telephone Number
  • Company Description
  • Business Hours
  • Social Media Links
  • Additional Contact Information

All updates remain subject to BizThai review.

Information That Cannot Be Modified

Verified companies generally may not modify:

  • Company Registration Number
  • Registered Company Name
  • Official Registration Status
  • Government Registration Records
  • Official Director Records
  • Historical Public Records
  • Government Filing Information

Changes to official records must first be completed with the relevant government authority.

Verification Review

BizThai may suspend, revoke or refuse verification if:

  • False information is submitted
  • Fraud is suspected
  • Documents cannot be verified
  • Verification is abused
  • Applicable laws require removal

Verification Fee

BizThai may provide verification services free of charge or as part of a paid subscription.

Fees, if applicable, will be published separately.

Limitation of Verification

Verification confirms only the identity or authority of the applicant.

Verification does not constitute an endorsement or guarantee of any company.

Users should continue conducting independent due diligence.
