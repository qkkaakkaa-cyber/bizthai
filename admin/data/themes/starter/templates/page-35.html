Data Correction & Removal Policy

How to request the correction, update or review of business information on BizThai

Effective Date: June 2026

Purpose

BizThai is committed to providing accurate, transparent and reliable business information to the public.

This Data Correction & Removal Policy explains how businesses, authorized representatives and other interested parties may request the correction, update or review of information displayed on the BizThai platform.

About BizThai

BizThai is an independent business information platform.

BizThai is not a government agency, not affiliated with the Department of Business Development (DBD) or any other government authority.

The information displayed on BizThai is collected, organized and presented from publicly available sources, official government records, publicly accessible databases and other lawful information sources.

Information Covered

Requests may be submitted regarding, but not limited to:

  • Company Name
  • Registration Number
  • Company Status
  • Registered Address
  • Business Activities
  • Directors
  • Shareholders (where legally available)
  • Contact Information
  • Website
  • Company Description
  • Company Logo
  • Duplicate Company Records
  • Incorrect Public Information

Who May Submit a Request

BizThai accepts requests from:

  • Company directors
  • Authorized representatives
  • Licensed lawyers
  • Government agencies
  • Court-appointed representatives
  • Other persons with sufficient legal authority

BizThai may request additional documents to verify the applicant's identity and authority.

Required Documents

Depending on the request, applicants may be required to provide:

  • Business Registration Certificate
  • Government-issued Identification
  • Letter of Authorization
  • Court Order
  • Official Government Documents
  • Evidence supporting the requested correction

BizThai reserves the right to request additional documentation.

How to Submit a Request

Please send all correction requests to:

Email
support@bizthai.com

Your request should include:

  • Company Name
  • Registration Number
  • Description of the issue
  • Requested correction
  • Supporting documents
  • Contact information

Incomplete requests may not be processed.

Review Process

Upon receiving a complete request, BizThai will:

  • Verify the submitted information;
  • Review supporting documents;
  • Compare the information with available public records;
  • Determine whether an update is appropriate;
  • Notify the applicant where applicable.

BizThai aims to review requests within 7–15 business days, although complex cases may require additional time.

Correction Requests

Where sufficient evidence is provided, BizThai may:

  • Correct inaccurate information;
  • Update outdated information;
  • Add missing public information;
  • Remove duplicate records;
  • Update company contact details;
  • Update company logos submitted by authorized representatives.

Removal Requests

BizThai generally does not remove information that originates from lawful public records solely because a company or individual prefers that such information not appear on the platform.

Public company information serves the public interest by promoting transparency and informed business decisions.

Removal requests may be considered only where:

  • Required by applicable law;
  • Ordered by a competent court;
  • Required by a government authority;
  • Information was published in error;
  • Information violates applicable privacy laws.

Information That Will Normally Not Be Removed

BizThai will generally not remove:

  • Company registration information;
  • Registration numbers;
  • Company status;
  • Official filing information;
  • Historical public records;
  • Information available from official public registries.

Data Sources

BizThai does not independently create official corporate registration records.

Information displayed may originate from:

  • Government registries;
  • Official publications;
  • Public databases;
  • Company submissions;
  • Other lawful public sources.

Official government records shall prevail in the event of any inconsistency.

No Guarantee

Although BizThai makes reasonable efforts to maintain accurate information, BizThai does not guarantee that all information is complete, current or error-free.

Users should verify important information directly with the relevant government authority before making legal, financial or commercial decisions.
