Business Data Usage Policy

How Business Information on BizThai May and May Not Be Used

Effective: June 2026

BizThai provides business information to promote transparency, support due diligence and facilitate informed business decisions.

This Business Data Usage Policy explains how information available on the BizThai platform may and may not be used.

Scope

This Policy applies to all company information, business records, search results, reports, directories, datasets and other business-related information displayed through the BizThai website, applications and related services.

Permitted Use

Users may use BizThai for legitimate purposes, including:

  • Company research
  • Supplier and partner verification
  • Legal entity status verification
  • Due diligence
  • Market research
  • Academic research
  • Personal reference

Use of BizThai must comply with applicable laws and these Terms of Use.

Prohibited Use

Without prior written permission from BizThai, users must not:

  • Copy substantial portions of the BizThai database
  • Download business information in bulk
  • Republish or redistribute BizThai data for commercial purposes
  • Mirror or reproduce the BizThai website
  • Use automated tools, bots or crawlers to collect data beyond reasonable browsing
  • Use BizThai data to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models
  • Sell, license or commercially exploit BizThai data
  • Circumvent technical measures intended to protect the platform

Public Records

Certain information displayed on BizThai originates from publicly available government or lawful public sources. BizThai organizes, indexes and enhances access to such information.

Nothing in this Policy limits any rights users may have under applicable laws regarding access to public records.

Company Submitted Information

Information voluntarily provided by companies remains subject to BizThai's review and publication policies.

Companies represent that they have the legal right to submit such information.

Data Accuracy

BizThai makes reasonable efforts to maintain accurate information but does not guarantee that all business information is complete, current or error-free.

Users should verify important information directly with the relevant government authority before making business decisions.

Intellectual Property

The BizThai database structure, search system, classifications, indexing, software, website design and other original content are protected by applicable intellectual property laws.

Unauthorized reproduction or commercial use may result in legal action.

API and Commercial Licensing

Businesses requiring large-scale access to BizThai data should obtain appropriate authorization or enter into a commercial licensing agreement where applicable.

BizThai reserves the right to suspend access, restrict services or pursue legal remedies against any person or organization that violates this Policy.
