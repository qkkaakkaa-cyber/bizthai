Data Sources Policy

Where the business information on BizThai comes from, how it is processed, and the principles governing its publication.

Effective date: June 2026

Purpose

BizThai is committed to providing transparent, reliable and lawfully obtained business information.

This Data Sources Policy explains where the information displayed on BizThai comes from, how it is processed, and the principles governing its publication.

About BizThai

BizThai is an independent business information platform.

BizThai is not a government agency and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of the Department of Business Development (DBD), the Ministry of Commerce of Thailand, or any other government authority unless expressly stated.

Sources of Information

Information displayed on BizThai may be collected from one or more lawful sources, including but not limited to:

  • Government business registries
  • Government Open Data platforms
  • Official public filings
  • Official Gazettes
  • Court publications
  • Regulatory authorities
  • Publicly available company websites
  • Information voluntarily submitted by companies
  • Licensed commercial data providers
  • Publicly accessible databases
  • Other lawful public information sources

Not every company profile will contain information from every source.

Government Records

Where information has been lawfully published by a competent government authority, BizThai may collect, organize, index, categorize and display such information to improve public accessibility and business transparency.

BizThai does not claim ownership of official government records.

Data Processing

To improve usability, BizThai may:

  • Organize public records;
  • Standardize company names;
  • Index registration numbers;
  • Merge duplicate records;
  • Improve searchability;
  • Translate selected information;
  • Associate related public records.

BizThai does not intentionally alter the meaning of official public records.

Company Submitted Information

Companies may voluntarily submit additional information including:

  • Company Logo
  • Website
  • Email Address
  • Telephone Number
  • Business Description
  • Social Media Links
  • Contact Information

BizThai reserves the right to review such submissions before publication.

Data Accuracy

BizThai makes reasonable efforts to maintain accurate and up-to-date information.

However, information may occasionally be:

  • Delayed;
  • Incomplete;
  • Outdated;
  • Subject to correction by the original source.

Where inconsistencies exist, official government records shall prevail.

Data Updates

BizThai periodically updates information from available lawful sources.

The frequency of updates may vary depending on:

  • Availability of source data;
  • Government publication schedules;
  • Technical limitations;
  • Internal review procedures.

BizThai does not guarantee real-time updates.

Correction Requests

If you believe information displayed on BizThai is inaccurate or incomplete, you may submit a correction request.

Email
support@bizthai.com

Requests will be reviewed in accordance with the BizThai Data Correction Policy.

Removal Requests

BizThai generally does not remove information originating from lawful public records solely because a company or individual prefers that such information not be displayed.

Removal requests may only be considered where:

  • Required by applicable law;
  • Ordered by a competent court;
  • Required by a government authority;
  • Information was published in error;
  • Required under applicable privacy legislation.

No Government Endorsement

The appearance of a company on BizThai does not indicate:

  • Government approval;
  • Government certification;
  • Government recommendation;
  • Company credibility;
  • Financial stability;
  • Legal compliance.

Users should conduct their own independent due diligence before making business decisions.

Future Data Sources

BizThai may expand its database by integrating additional lawful public data sources from Thailand and other jurisdictions.

This Policy may be updated from time to time without prior notice.
