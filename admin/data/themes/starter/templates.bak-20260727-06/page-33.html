Copyright Policy

How copyright owners may report alleged infringement on BizThai

Effective Date: June 2026

Introduction

BizThai respects the intellectual property rights of others and is committed to complying with applicable copyright, trademark and other intellectual property laws.

This Copyright Policy explains how copyright owners may report alleged infringement and how BizThai handles such requests.

Respect for Intellectual Property

BizThai expects all users, companies and content providers to respect the intellectual property rights of others.

Users must not submit, upload or distribute any material that infringes the copyright, trademark or other proprietary rights of any third party.

Content Displayed on BizThai

Content displayed on BizThai may include:

  • Company names
  • Company logos
  • Business descriptions
  • Contact information
  • Public registration information
  • Images submitted by verified companies
  • Publicly available information obtained from lawful sources

BizThai does not claim ownership of third-party intellectual property.

Copyright Complaints

If you believe that copyrighted material has been displayed on BizThai without proper authorization, you may submit a copyright complaint. Please send your notice to:

Email
support@bizthai.com

Your complaint should include:

  • Your full name
  • Company name (if applicable)
  • Contact information
  • Description of the copyrighted work
  • URL(s) of the allegedly infringing content
  • Evidence of ownership or authorization
  • A statement confirming that the information provided is accurate

Incomplete requests may delay processing.

Review Procedure

Upon receiving a valid copyright complaint, BizThai may:

  • Review the submitted materials;
  • Request additional supporting documents;
  • Temporarily restrict access to the reported content where appropriate;
  • Contact the relevant parties;
  • Remove or retain the content based on applicable law and available evidence.

BizThai aims to review copyright complaints within 7–15 business days.

Company Logos and Trademarks

Company logos and trademarks displayed on BizThai may originate from:

  • Official company submissions;
  • Publicly available sources;
  • Lawful public records.

If a logo or trademark is displayed incorrectly or without authorization, the rights holder may request a review by contacting support@bizthai.com.

Counter-Notice

If content has been removed or restricted following a copyright complaint and you believe the action was made in error, you may submit a counter-notice with supporting evidence.

BizThai will review the counter-notice in accordance with applicable law.

Repeat Infringement

BizThai reserves the right to restrict, suspend or terminate access for users or organizations that repeatedly submit infringing material or abuse the copyright complaint process.

No Guarantee

Submission of a copyright complaint does not automatically result in the removal of content.

BizThai will evaluate each request individually based on the available evidence, applicable law and the nature of the content.
