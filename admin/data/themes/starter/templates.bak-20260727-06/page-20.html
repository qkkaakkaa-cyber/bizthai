About

Thailand's Business Intelligence Platform

BizThai is a business intelligence platform focused on Thailand.

We bring together company information, business data, industry insights, and market intelligence to help businesses access reliable information, identify opportunities, and make informed decisions.

Whether you are researching a company, evaluating a supplier, exploring an industry, or entering the Thai market, BizThai provides the information you need to move forward with confidence.

What We Offer

BizThai provides a growing range of business information and digital services, including:

  • Company profiles and registration records
  • Directors and management information
  • Company status
  • Industry classifications and business directories
  • Business news and market updates
  • Supplier and buyer directories
  • Business intelligence and credit references
  • Industry research and market insights
  • APIs and enterprise data solutions

Who We Serve

BizThai serves businesses, investors, procurement teams, financial institutions, legal and accounting professionals, researchers, entrepreneurs, government organizations, and companies doing business in Thailand.

Our Data

Our platform organizes information collected from publicly available government records, company disclosures, and other legally accessible sources.

We continuously improve the quality, coverage, and accessibility of our data to provide reliable business information for our users.

Looking Ahead

BizThai is more than a company information platform.

We are building a comprehensive business ecosystem that connects company data, market intelligence, supplier discovery, industry information, and digital business services, helping organizations connect, grow, and succeed in Thailand.
